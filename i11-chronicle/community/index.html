Agency Support Group Meeting

Monthly Agency Support Group Meeting intended to provide a collaborative environment for federal agency security professionals to receive direct support and guidance on FedRAMP certification efforts. This meeting is only open to federal agencies. If you are interested in joining please reach out to info@fedramp.gov for more information. Purpose: To provide a collaborative, open-forum environment where agency practitioners can receive direct support from the FedRAMP team on adopting 20x and ensuring Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) are successfully implementing Balance Improvement Release (BIR) plans. Outcomes: - Provide agencies with actionable answers and guidance on specific FedRAMP packages currently undergoing authorization. - Assist practitioners with technical and process questions regarding the transition of systems to one or more BIRs. - Foster shared learning and problem-solving across different agency teams. Note: This is an optional, recurring working session meant to supplement the standard agency liaison meetings. It is only open to federal agencies and not the general public. If you work in a federal agency and have questions about implementation of FedRAMP (Rev5 or 20x) and would like to be added to this meeting please contact info@fedramp.gov for assistance.

EventsSep 8, 2026

Agency Liaison Meeting

Monthly Agency Liaison Meeting intended to brief FedRAMP Agency Liaisons on changes, updates, and relevant information that directly affects federal agencies. This meeting is only open to federal agencies. If you are interested in joining please reach out to info@fedramp.gov for more information. Purpose: To serve as a dedicated touchpoint where FedRAMP Agency Liaisons can engage directly with the FedRAMP team to discuss program updates, share agency-specific challenges, and collaborate on improving the authorization process for cloud services across the federal government. Outcomes: - Keep agency liaisons informed of the latest FedRAMP policy changes, process improvements, and program initiatives that impact their agency's cloud adoption efforts. - Build a community of practice among agency liaisons to share best practices, lessons learned, and successful strategies for FedRAMP implementation. - Gather feedback from agencies to inform future FedRAMP program improvements and policy development. Note: This meeting series is exclusively for designated FedRAMP Agency Liaisons and their authorized delegates within federal agencies. It serves as the primary communication channel between agencies and FedRAMP. If you are a federal employee interested in becoming your agency's FedRAMP liaison or need to update your agency's liaison contact information, please reach out to info@fedramp.gov.

RFC 0030 · FedRAMP Rev5 Security Controls Baseline Update for RA, SA, SC, SI, and SR Control Families

This RFC proposes updates to the Additional FedRAMP Security Technical Controls Requirements and Guidance for FedRAMP Rev5 baselines in the Risk Assessment (RA), System and Service Acquisition (SA), System and Communications Protection (SC), System and Information Integrity (SI), and Supply Chain Risk Management (SR) control families. The updates are intended to refresh outdated guidance to match current FedRAMP rules and lower the compliance burden for cloud service providers.
