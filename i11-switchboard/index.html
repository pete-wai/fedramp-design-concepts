Your Agency.
Your decision.

FedRAMP ensures you have the information you need to make the right decisions for your agency mission, and supplies the agency use framework to simplify cloud adoption while meeting existing legal and regulatory requirements.

  • A government-wide foundation. The FedRAMP Authorization Act directs GSA to support reusable cloud security assessments, guidance, and continuous monitoring.
  • Reuse the work. OMB M-24-15 directs agencies to treat FedRAMP authorization materials as presumptively adequate for the provider's security assessment.
  • Own your use decision. Your agency applies its FISMA responsibilities to its own information system, configuration, mission needs, and risk.
