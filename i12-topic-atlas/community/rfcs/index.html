This RFC proposes updates to the Additional FedRAMP Security Technical Controls Requirements and Guidance for FedRAMP Rev5 baselines in the Risk Assessment (RA), System and Service Acquisition (SA), System and Communications Protection (SC), System and Information Integrity (SI), and Supply Chain Risk Management (SR) control families.
