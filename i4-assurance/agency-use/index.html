FedRAMP 20x

Your agency. Your decision.

FedRAMP Certification gives agencies reusable security evidence. Your agency decides whether and how to authorize a service for its mission.

Begin with the intended use.

Define what the service will do, the information involved and your agency’s requirements. Certification is evidence for your decision, not a blanket authorization to operate.

Agency-use overview

Review the package in context.

  1. Check the offering’s certification status, class and scope.
  2. Review its package and current evidence. Content varies by class.
  3. Evaluate configuration guidance and responsibilities for your intended use.
  4. Document your agency’s assessment, authorization and monitoring arrangements.

Agencies SHOULD NOT authorize Class A use beyond 12 months unless the offering is actively seeking B, C or D.
