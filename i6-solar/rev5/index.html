FedRAMP 20x

Rev5 is moving toward 20x.

If you already work with Rev5, make the transition part of your planning now.

Requirements still apply.

Rev5 providers must meet applicable Consolidated Rules and phased adoption deadlines, including ongoing certification obligations. Read the current requirements alongside the transition guidance.

Read what is changing · Legacy reference

New applications and existing certifications have different timelines.

FedRAMP stops accepting new Rev5 certification applications on June 11, 2027. Existing Rev5 certifications are expected to remain active until at least December 31, 2028, unless FedRAMP is otherwise directed, subject to ongoing obligations.

That is not a universal December 2028 expiry date. Check the current deadlines before making a transition plan.
