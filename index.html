A clearer day.

Twelve approaches to federal assurance designed for commercial cloud. Familiar words and distinct layouts, explored in soft blue and white—with a contrasting dark mode for every site.

Design prototypes, not the official government service. Marketplace and RFC pages contain preserved demo material. Certification timing is a goal for prepared teams, subject to readiness and FedRAMP review.
