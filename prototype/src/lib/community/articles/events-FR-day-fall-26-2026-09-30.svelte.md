<h2>About this update</h2>
<p>We&#x27;re ending the year with a bang! FedRAMP received an invitation to take over the second day of the annual 2026 Cloud Service Providers – Advisory Board (CSP-AB) Summit. Our final FedRAMP Day for federal fiscal year 2026 will be an open invitation to all FedRAMP stakeholders instead of having an exclusive event for federal agencies. Attendees will get the latest updates from the FedRAMP leadership team, live and in-person. Here from these FedRAMP speakers throughout the day: * Paul Agosta, FedRAMP Cloud Security Officer and Rev5 Lead * Dan Chandler, FedRAMP Cloud Security Engineer * Elisha &quot;Emu&quot; Crow, FedRAMP Cloud Security Officer and 20x Lead * Ryan Hoesing, FedRAMP Chief of Staff * Nicole Thompson, FedRAMP Security Director * Pete Waterman, FedRAMP Director Nearly 20 FedRAMP team members from across the nation will be available to engage with all summit attendees. The final agenda is coming soon, but attendees can expect to learn more about the road ahead for FedRAMP, details about 20x Phase 4, the Rev5 Class D pipeline, and more. Registration Information Learn more about the summit and how to register: https://www.csp-ab.com/csp-ab-announces-2026-two-day-summit-featuring-a-fedramp-day-open-to-the-full-stakeholder-community/</p>
<p>Scheduled time: 9:30 AM–4:00 PM Eastern Time.</p>
