<h2>About this update</h2>
<p>Monthly Agency Liaison Meeting intended to brief FedRAMP Agency Liaisons on changes, updates, and relevant information that directly affects federal agencies. This meeting is only open to federal agencies. If you are interested in joining please reach out to info@fedramp.gov for more information. Purpose: To serve as a dedicated touchpoint where FedRAMP Agency Liaisons can engage directly with the FedRAMP team to discuss program updates, share agency-specific challenges, and collaborate on improving the authorization process for cloud services across the federal government. Outcomes: - Keep agency liaisons informed of the latest FedRAMP policy changes, process improvements, and program initiatives that impact their agency&#x27;s cloud adoption efforts. - Build a community of practice among agency liaisons to share best practices, lessons learned, and successful strategies for FedRAMP implementation. - Gather feedback from agencies to inform future FedRAMP program improvements and policy development. Note: This meeting series is exclusively for designated FedRAMP Agency Liaisons and their authorized delegates within federal agencies. It serves as the primary communication channel between agencies and FedRAMP. If you are a federal employee interested in becoming your agency&#x27;s FedRAMP liaison or need to update your agency&#x27;s liaison contact information, please reach out to info@fedramp.gov.</p>
<p>Scheduled time: 11:00 AM–12:00 PM Eastern Time.</p>
<p>2nd Tuesday of Every Month</p>
