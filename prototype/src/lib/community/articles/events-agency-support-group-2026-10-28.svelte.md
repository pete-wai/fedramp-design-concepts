<h2>About this update</h2>
<p>Monthly Agency Support Group Meeting intended to provide a collaborative environment for federal agency security professionals to receive direct support and guidance on FedRAMP certification efforts. This meeting is only open to federal agencies. If you are interested in joining please reach out to info@fedramp.gov for more information. Purpose: To provide a collaborative, open-forum environment where agency practitioners can receive direct support from the FedRAMP team on adopting 20x and ensuring Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) are successfully implementing Balance Improvement Release (BIR) plans. Outcomes: - Provide agencies with actionable answers and guidance on specific FedRAMP packages currently undergoing authorization. - Assist practitioners with technical and process questions regarding the transition of systems to one or more BIRs. - Foster shared learning and problem-solving across different agency teams. Note: This is an optional, recurring working session meant to supplement the standard agency liaison meetings. It is only open to federal agencies and not the general public. If you work in a federal agency and have questions about implementation of FedRAMP (Rev5 or 20x) and would like to be added to this meeting please contact info@fedramp.gov for assistance.</p>
<p>Scheduled time: 1:00 PM–2:00 PM Eastern Time.</p>
<p>4th Wednesday of Every Month</p>
