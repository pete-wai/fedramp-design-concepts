<h2>From the official source</h2>
<p>Q: How should cloud service providers transition from the traditional POA&amp;M process to the new VDR/VER process?</p>
<p>A: Inform your agencies of the planned changes before they happen. If you have a continuous monitoring charter, update your charter to reflect the changes in process, documentation, and meeting periodicity. Outline expectations for all parties. Do not email FedRAMP to let us know.</p>
