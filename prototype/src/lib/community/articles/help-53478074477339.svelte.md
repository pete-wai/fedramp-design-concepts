<h2>From the official source</h2>
<p>Q: With the release of the consolidated rules, we&#x27;ve noted that new rev 5 certifications will not be accepted after June 2027. Can you please confirm that while net-new rev 5 certifications will not be accepted after June 2027, uplifts of existing Moderate/Class C offerings will still be accepted?</p>
<p>A: FedRAMP treats uplifts as net new Authorization and no Rev 5 uplifts will be accepted after the 11 June 2027 date.</p>
