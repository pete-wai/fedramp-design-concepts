<h2>From the official source</h2>
<p>Q: What milestone must be reached by June 2027 for a new authorization effort to proceed under Rev. 5</p>
<p>A: Your package must be in active review by the FedRAMP PMO by 11 June 2027. You must have a complete FedRAMP Package submitted to the FedRAMP PMO. This includes an active Agency ATO unless you are part of the limited sponsorless path. This also includes you meeting CR26 requirements at the time of submission. Those requirements and their timeframes can be found at the link below. https://www.fedramp.gov/2026/providers/updating/deadlines/rev5/</p>
<p>Rev5 submissions to FedRAMP after 11 June 2027 will not be accepted. If you are currently in Agency review or planning to enter soon, it is imperative that you make sure your schedule and your agency review timelines aligns with the 11 June timeframe.</p>
