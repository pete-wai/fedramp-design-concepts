<h2>From the official source</h2>
<p>Q: Which external frameworks are accepted for Class A?</p>
<p>A: The CSP must have completed an eligible certification or equivalent process within the preceding 12 months :</p>
<p>FedRAMP Rev. 5, including FedRAMP Ready, at any historical impact level</p>
<p>SOC 2 Type II</p>
<p>GovRAMP at any impact level</p>
<p>For a SOC 2 Type II submission, provide the complete report, any applicable bridge or gap letter, verified audit-engagement documentation, the estimated schedule for the next report, and supplemental compliance evidence where applicable.</p>
