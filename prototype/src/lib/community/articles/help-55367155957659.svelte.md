<h2>From the official source</h2>
<p>Q: Is “see the SOC 2 report” an acceptable response to FedRAMP rule?</p>
<p>A: Usually, no. FedRAMP states that the Class A package should contain enough detail for a reviewer to understand the security decision without having to dig through the external-framework materials. Map the SOC 2 evidence to the relevant FedRAMP rule, explain your implementation, and link directly to the supporting artifact.</p>
