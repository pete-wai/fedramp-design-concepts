<h2>From the official source</h2>
<p>Q: Can we include historical KSI metrics in Class A?</p>
<p>A: Yes, but it is optional for Class A. CSPs may include historical KSI metrics in the SDR. If included, the metrics should be clearly scoped, dated, attributable to the stated service, and linked to the relevant KSI or rule.</p>
