<h2>From the official source</h2>
<p>Q: Can our assessor, consultant, or advisory firm submit the application for us?</p>
<p>A: No. A CSP must not use a third party to apply for certification on its behalf, including an independent assessment service. Third parties may help prepare the materials, but the CSP must submit the application directly and remain accountable for it. Source: fedramp</p>
